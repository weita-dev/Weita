Frequently asked questions

What does WeiTA do for our organisation?

WeiTA builds and runs your organisation's own standing talent community, keeps it engaged all year, and brings it together with your leaders whenever a capability is needed.

How is this different from an agency or an RPO?

WeiTA builds a community your organisation owns and engages ahead of any need, then convenes it with your leaders in the Talent Connect room. Each engagement leaves the community stronger for the next.

How soon do we see results?

The community starts forming in the first month, and the first Talent Connect room can run within the first quarter. Progress is measured against your own baseline.

Who owns the community?

Your organisation. WeiTA builds and runs it on the WeiTA platform, and your organisation keeps access at all times.

How do we start?

Share the capability your organisation needs next. The first conversation sets the scope.

What is an evergreen talent ecosystem?

Your organisation's own standing talent community: professionals engaged all year, before a need, during it and after it. WeiTA uses both terms for the same thing.

Does WeiTA replace our TA team or partners?

WeiTA works alongside your TA team and existing partners, and changes the process they run.

How much leader time does it take?

One full day in the Talent Connect room, in place of weeks of interview panels.

Which organisations does WeiTA work with?

GCCs, enterprises and startups across India.

Can we start small?

Yes. An engagement can start with one critical capability, one city or one new centre, and widen from there.

How are results measured?

WeiTA agrees your baseline numbers at the start, such as time to hire, cost per hire and offer-to-join, and reports progress against them.

Why do professionals stay engaged?

Every touchpoint gives something back: learning, access to leaders, visibility, recognition and a network.
