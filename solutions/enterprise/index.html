Where talent acquisition falls behind the business plan

  1. Talent acquisition strategy follows the requisition.

    The plan names the skills it needs a year ahead; talent work begins once each role is approved.

  2. Time to hire stretches on niche and senior roles.

    Specialist and leadership roles stay open across quarters while the plan’s milestones keep their dates.

  3. Every role and every exit restarts the search.

    Each requisition returns to portals and agencies, and the market is rebuilt from the first name.

  4. Employer brand reaches talent through job advertisements.

    Professionals hear of the organisation when a role opens, and talent leans toward employers it already knows.

  5. Quality of hire rests on a late, filtered view.

    The leaders best placed to judge see a final shortlist late, and decide under time pressure.

Frequently asked questions

What is a talent acquisition strategy?

A plan for how the organisation finds, engages and secures the skills its business plan needs. WeiTA builds it from the business plan, ahead of the requisition.

How does WeiTA reduce time to hire for niche roles?

Professionals are already in conversation with your leaders when a role opens, so the decision starts from a relationship well under way.

How does WeiTA strengthen our employer brand?

Through the year, professionals come to know your leaders, problems and plans through articles, roundtables and Build Days. Your organisation is known well before a role opens.

How does WeiTA improve quality of hire?

Leaders come to know professionals over months of conversation and a working day on a live problem. Decisions rest on work seen and fit understood on both sides.

What is a Talent Connect blueprint?

WeiTA's design for your organisation: which modules run, for which capabilities, and in what order, drawn from your business plan.

How does a talent strategy cover AI, cloud and cyber skills?

Talent Connect Emerging Skills runs continuously, so your leaders keep standing access to specialists as these skills evolve.

How does a GCC build its team in a new Indian city?

Talent Connect City and Leadership Bench map the city's skills, make your leaders known locally, and convene a founding-team Talent Connect room.

What happens after the pilot?

The blueprint extends to the next capabilities in the plan, and the talent ecosystem keeps growing.

What does a Talent Connect blueprint cost?

Fees are set in each proposal, by modules and capabilities.

Can WeiTA help with a role that has stayed open for months?

Yes. A long-open role is a good place to start. WeiTA maps where that capability sits and brings the right professionals into a Talent Connect room within 15 days.
