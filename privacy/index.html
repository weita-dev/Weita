Privacy Notice

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This page explains, in plain words, what details WeiTA collects, why, who sees them, how long WeiTA keeps them, and how you stay in control. Pick the part that fits you. The "For everyone" section applies to all readers.

You can read this notice in English, or in any language listed in the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution of India, such as Hindi, Kannada or Tamil. Write to privacy@weita.in and WeiTA will send it in your language.

Website visitor

What WeiTA collects
  • From every visit: your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you open and the time of your visit. These are recorded in the website's server logs.
  • Only if you choose "Accept" on the cookie bar: anonymous visit statistics from Google Analytics, such as pages viewed, time on page, rough location (city level) and how you found the site.
Why
  • Server logs keep the website running safely and help WeiTA fix problems.
  • Visit statistics show WeiTA which pages help visitors most, so the site can get clearer.
Who sees it
  • Krishna Kumar, WeiTA's founder.
  • GoDaddy, which hosts the website, and Google, which runs Analytics, Fonts and reCAPTCHA. Each works under its own security and privacy terms.
How long it is kept
  • Server logs: on GoDaddy's standard log cycle, and at least one year, as Indian law asks.
  • Visit statistics: until Google Analytics clears them automatically on its rolling schedule. You can stop them at any time through "Cookie settings" in the footer.

Organisation

What WeiTA collects
  • From the client form: full name, work email, phone number, job title, organisation, organisation website, city, organisation type (Enterprise or Foundry), what you want help with, and your consent.
  • If you use "Book a direct call": your name, email, the time you choose and anything you add to the booking.
Why
  • WeiTA uses these details only to reply to you, understand what your organisation needs, set up and hold the call, and run any engagement you agree to.
  • Your email address also receives one confirmation that WeiTA has your request.
Who sees it
  • Krishna Kumar and the WeiTA team who handle your request. The form alert goes to sales@weita.in.
  • Google stores the details for WeiTA (Sheets, Gmail, Calendar and Meet) under its security terms.
How long it is kept
  • For as long as the conversation or engagement continues. When it ends, WeiTA reviews your details and deletes them.
  • WeiTA keeps only what contract or tax law requires, and a basic record of the form submission for at least one year, as Indian law asks.

Partner

What WeiTA collects
  • From the partner form: full name, email, phone number, organisation (optional), role, city, website or LinkedIn (optional), which best describes you (including your own words if you choose "Other"), and your consent.
Why
  • WeiTA uses these details only to reply to you, explore working together, and stay in touch about the partnership you discuss.
  • Your email address also receives one confirmation that WeiTA has your message.
Who sees it
  • Krishna Kumar, WeiTA's founder, who receives the form alert directly.
  • Google stores the details for WeiTA (Sheets and Gmail) under its security terms.
How long it is kept
  • For as long as the partnership conversation or the partnership continues. When it ends, WeiTA reviews your details and deletes them.
  • A basic record of the form submission stays for at least one year, as Indian law asks.

Career applicant

What WeiTA collects
  • From the career form: full name, email, phone number, city, LinkedIn profile, current role, current organisation, the role that interests you, your project walkthrough, why WeiTA (optional), your CV file, a cover letter (optional), and your consent.
Why
  • WeiTA uses these details only to consider you for roles at WeiTA, contact you about them and arrange conversations.
  • Your email address also receives one confirmation that WeiTA has your application.
Who sees it
  • Krishna Kumar and the people at WeiTA who take part in the decision for the role. The form alert goes to career@weita.in.
  • Your CV and cover letter are saved in a private Google Drive folder that only the WeiTA team for that role can open.
  • Your profile stays within WeiTA and is used only for roles at WeiTA.
How long it is kept
  • While WeiTA considers you for roles. You can ask WeiTA to delete your details at any time, and WeiTA will do so.
  • If you join WeiTA, your details move into your employee records. A basic record of the form submission stays for at least one year, as Indian law asks.

For everyone

Who is responsible

WeiTA decides how your details are used. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, WeiTA is the "Data Fiduciary". WeiTA is run by its founder, Krishna Kumar, who also acts as Grievance Officer.

Where details are kept

Form details sit in Google Sheets, CV files in a private Google Drive folder, and emails in Gmail, all inside WeiTA's Google Workspace account. Access is limited to the people named in each part above, with encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Google may store these details in data centres outside India, under Google's security commitments and as Indian law allows.

Services WeiTA uses

  • Google Workspace: Sheets (form entries), Drive (CV files), Gmail (emails), Apps Script (form processing), Calendar and Meet ("Book a direct call").
  • Google Analytics: visit statistics, only after you choose "Accept".
  • Google reCAPTCHA: keeps the forms free of spam. Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.
  • Google Fonts: shows the website's typefaces.
  • GoDaddy: hosts the website files and server logs.

Your rights

Write to privacy@weita.in to use any of these rights. WeiTA replies within one month.

  • See your detailsAsk for a summary of what WeiTA holds and how it is used.
  • Correct themFix, complete or update your details.
  • Delete themAsk WeiTA to erase your details.
  • Withdraw consentAs easy as giving it: one email to privacy@weita.in, or "Cookie settings" for Analytics. WeiTA then stops using those details. What was done before you withdrew stays valid.
  • Name someoneChoose a person to use these rights for you if you die or become unable to.
  • Raise a concernWeiTA's Grievance Officer answers within one month, inside the time Indian law sets.
  • Complain to the Data Protection BoardIf you are unhappy with WeiTA's answer, you can take your complaint to the Data Protection Board of India.

Cookies

Google Analytics cookies run only if you choose "Accept" on the cookie bar. You can change your choice any time through "Cookie settings" in the footer. The forms use one reCAPTCHA cookie to tell people from spam bots; the forms need it to work.

Limits

  • WeiTA's website and forms are meant for adults aged 18 and over.
  • WeiTA asks only for work details. Please leave health, financial, identity-number or password details out of your messages and CV.
  • Links to other websites follow those websites' own privacy notices.
